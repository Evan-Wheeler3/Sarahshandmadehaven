Sarah's Handmade Haven is my little corner for making things by hand. It started with a sewing machine, a pile of fabric scraps, and a stack of books that needed protecting.

Book covers are what I'm making right now, but other handmade goodies turn up from time to time. Follow along on Instagram so you don't miss them.
